48-Hour Return Window

Your return window opens the moment your order is marked as delivered. You have 48 hours from that point to request a return.

✔

What We Accept Returns For

  • Item significantly different from the photos or description on the product page
  • Undisclosed damage, stains, or defects not mentioned when you ordered
  • Wrong item received
  • Item arrived damaged during shipping
✖

What We Don't Accept Returns For

  • Change of mind after purchase
  • Sizing issues, please check the measurements in the product description carefully before ordering
  • Minor wear consistent with how the item was described
  • Return requests made after the 48-hour window has closed
🔄

How to Request a Return

01

Message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of your order being marked delivered, with your order number.

02

Describe the issue and attach clear photos of the item and any damage or defect.

03

We review your request within 24 hours and let you know if it's approved, along with return shipping instructions.

04

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, your refund is processed within 5-7 business days.

Questions about a return?

If you're unsure whether your issue qualifies, reach out before your window closes.
